Trait Dial

I believe there's an ever-growing, ever-changing human personality underneath all of this, and personality quizzes are a low-dimensional reading of it. This page is my attempt to understand the shape of that thing as much as I can.

One underlying Big Five profile, three stereotype readouts. Turn the OCEAN sliders, flip an MBTI switch, or pick an Enneagram archetype: whichever knob you touch rewrites the profile, and the other two panels update to match. Folk-psychology mapping, not a validated instrument.
