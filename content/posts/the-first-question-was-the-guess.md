Episode 2 of our Twenty Questions series.
We hid the problem a recent paper raised and showed an AI only the field as it stood before that paper.
Asking 20 yes/no questions found the problem in 12 of 15 games, and listing 10 guesses found it in 5.
Then we noticed the asker's first question was usually already the right guess.
The field text, written by a model that had read the paper, had pointed at the belief the paper breaks.
