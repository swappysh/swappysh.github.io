We gave an AI the plan we wrote on the first day of a project.
Then we let it hunt for the idea our paper landed on about two weeks later.
In ten games it found that idea once, on its very last guess.
A map of the field and a team of models to suggest questions barely helped.
The hard step was leaving the frame our plan started in.
