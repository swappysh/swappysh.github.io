Episode 2 of our Twenty Questions series.
We hid the problem a recent paper raised and showed an AI only the field as it stood before that paper.
In the first run, asking yes/no questions found it in 12 of 15 games, but the field text had been written by a model that had read the paper.
With the field written blind, asking found it in 4 of 15 games and listing 10 guesses found it in none.
Asking with coin-flip answers found it in none of 30, so the finds came from the answers.
