## Hiking & trails

Mostly Peninsula and SF paths so far, good for a long walk or a bike:

- Sawyer Camp Trail (Peninsula, paved, along San Andreas Lake)
- Bay Trail (Foster City / Redwood City stretches, flat, easy pace)
- Crissy Field → Fort Point (SF waterfront, ends at the Golden Gate Bridge)
- Cañada Road (car-free Sundays, along Crystal Springs Reservoir)
- Angel Island (ferry out, full loop, old bunkers and bay overlooks)
